01From idea to chain

four calls, one click
1. createChain()

Name it and pick a gas token. New or an existing SPL token.

2. configure()

Set fees, block time, validators and who can deploy programs.

3. pickModules()

Choose settlement, data availability, RPC and bridges.

4. deploy()

Your chain goes live with an explorer, RPC and dashboard.

06Where fees come from

        

          
Chain deployment
A one-time fee to launch a chain.

          
Infrastructure subscriptions
Managed validators, RPC and DA, billed monthly.

          
Marketplace commissions
A cut of trades and launches in the chain marketplace.

          
Shared sequencer fees
Paid per transaction ordered.

          
$SVM discounts
Pay any of the above in $SVM for a lower price.
